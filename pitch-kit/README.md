# Pitch kit

Everything you need to approach a company, run the first call, quote the work and follow up.
Nothing here is on the website — it is the material you send, say and hand over.

Fill in the placeholders once and the rest works:

| Placeholder | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `[YOUR NAME]` | Your name |
| `[PHONE]` | The number you want business calls on |
| `[EMAIL]` | Your business email |
| `[SITE]` | Your website address |

## The order it goes in

```
approach ──> 15-min call ──> ₹9,999 audit ──> written report ──> fixed-price quote ──> build
  (01)         (03)                              (04)               (05)
```

Two deliberate features of that path. The audit is a small paid step, so nobody has to trust a
large number before you have seen their work — and you never quote a job you have not watched.
And each step is independently useful: a client who stops after the report has still got
something worth ₹9,999, which is what makes the report worth writing properly.

## The rule for all of it

Every document here is written to be **specific, costed and falsifiable**. No "streamline your
operations", no "digital transformation", no "10x productivity". You are selling to people who
have been sold software before and are tired.

The strongest thing you have is the sentence nobody else says: **"that job is not worth
automating."** Say it early and often. It is the reason everything else you say gets believed.
