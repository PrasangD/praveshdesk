## Notes for you, not for them

- **Print in black and white.** It will be photocopied and passed around; do not depend on colour.
- **Write on it before handing it over.** Circle the row in the first table that matches what they
  just told you, and put their number next to it. A marked-up page survives a desk drawer; a clean
  one does not.
- **Leave two copies.** One for them, one for the person they will need to convince.
- **The price list is the point.** Most people in this market will not tell you their prices. Being
  the one who does is disproportionately persuasive, and it filters out the people who were never
  going to buy.
